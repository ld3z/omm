<Panel title="Something to know">
  Before installing the userscript, you'll need a UserScript manager to manage it.
</Panel>

:::warning[Note:]
These steps are for Mac only!
:::

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the UserScripts app">
  Install the Userscripts app from the Apple App Store.

  <DownloadButton href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/userscripts/id1463298887" icon="puzzle" secondary>Get Userscripts</DownloadButton>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the app & configure Safari">
  Open the **```Settings```** app on your Mac and find Safari. Look under extensions, and find **```Userscripts```** then enable it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the userscript">
  Install the OhMyMathpad userscript.

  <DownloadButton href="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ld3z/omm/main/oh-my-mathpad.user.js">Install userscript</DownloadButton>
  </Step>
</Steps>
